# 🥚 EggMemo

### Your little knowledge vault.

EggMemo is a personal knowledge bookmark app developed by **eateggslab**. It helps you save, organize, and revisit interesting content from across the internet — all in one place.

Save the things you love. Keep the knowledge you find. Revisit it whenever you need.

---

## ✨ About EggMemo

EggMemo is designed to make collecting and organizing digital content simple and convenient.

Whether it's a YouTube video, an Instagram reel, a useful link, or something you want to remember for later, EggMemo helps you keep it organized in your personal library.

### Features

- 🔖 **Save Gems** — Save interesting links and online content for later.
- 📚 **Collections** — Organize saved content into collections that suit your interests.
- 🧠 **Personal Knowledge Vault** — Keep useful content in one place.
- 🎨 **Personalization** — Customize your app experience.
- 📝 **Sketches** — Create and save sketches within the app.
- 💾 **Local Storage** — Keep your saved content and app data stored locally on your device.
