EggMemo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

EggMemo is a personal knowledge bookmark application developed by eateggslab. This Privacy Policy explains how EggMemo handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Store

EggMemo is designed to store your saved content and preferences locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Saved links and URLs
  • Titles and descriptions of saved content
  • Collection names
  • Downloaded thumbnails and images
  • App preferences and personalization settings
  • Sketches created within the app

2. Local Storage

Your saved content is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage system.

3. Internet Access

EggMemo may access the internet to retrieve webpage information, previews, titles, descriptions, or images when you save a link.

The content you choose to access through external websites is governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing

EggMemo does not operate its own account system or cloud service for storing your saved content.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete saved content within the application. Uninstalling the application may also remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and backup settings.

6. Children's Privacy

EggMemo does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
